# Code of Conduct

PyLeanX follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be welcoming and patient (many users are mathematicians or scientists new to
formal proof, others are new to mathematics), critique ideas rather than people, and assume
good faith. Harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory language are not tolerated.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers through
[GitHub's private reporting](https://github.com/vizpageai/pyleanx/security/advisories/new)
or by contacting a maintainer directly. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers
may remove contributions and temporarily or permanently ban participants who violate this
code, as described in the Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
